# Safety and confirmation policy

Before a destructive, financial, account, privacy-sensitive, or irreversible operation, state the exact command family, target, and expected effect, then obtain confirmation. This applies to app/file mutations, permission changes, camera/microphone access, notification open/action/dismiss operations, location changes, and account or purchase flows.

Do not expose secrets, helper tokens, raw media, full logs, or unbounded DOM/device output in chat. Use compact proof, SHA-256 metadata, and artifact paths. Clean up only resources AU created and tracked. Never infer permission to alter unrelated apps, settings, forwards, reverses, processes, or files.
